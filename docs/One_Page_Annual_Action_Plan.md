# Strategic Commercial Action Plan (2026): GCC TAVI & Navitor Expansion

**Goal:** Accelerate adoption of Abbott’s Navitor™ TAVI system across high-volume tertiary hubs in Saudi Arabia and the GCC by expanding into low-to-intermediate risk indications, optimizing NUPCO framework agreements, and elevating Heart Team clinical engagement.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Priorities

| Priority | Objective | Primary Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P1: NUPCO Listing & Access** | Expand Navitor SKU presence on the NUPCO electronic catalogue for seamless MOH/Military procurement in KSA. | MOH & National Guard Health Affairs (NGHA) |
| **P2: Low-Risk Cohort Activation** | Leverage CE Mark expanded indications to transition intermediate-risk AS patients to TAVI. | Key Tertiary Cardiac Centers (KSMC, KFMC, KFSH&RC) |
| **P3: Heart Team Clinical Support** | Provide specialized proctorship, imaging analysis support, and inventory optimization for Cath Labs. | Interventional Cardiologists & Cardiac Surgeons |
